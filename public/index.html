rugscore

Deterministic creator-reputation gate for pump.fun graduations. No LLM, no black box — the same threshold the live trading bot uses, exposed as a lookup.

Methodology. Every pump.fun token's creator wallet is tagged at mint time: how many tokens they launched before (priorCount), whether this was a batch-deploy (<60s after another launch by the same wallet), whether one of their prior tokens already graduated (priorGrad), and how much SOL they bought of their own token at creation (devBuySol).

On 11,495 real graduations (two independent date-split halves), the gate prior==0 & devBuy≤0.5 SOL & no batch separates EV +13.1% / +7.5% from +4.3% / +6.4% for the rest. A creator whose prior token already graduated gets a further 5.3x lift in graduation rate within that gate.

Separately, on rug-pull dynamics: 32% of graduated tokens rug within 1 hour, 85% never reach 2x, and when a rug happens, 71% go to zero within 30 seconds — near-zero reaction time once it starts, which is why the gate matters more than any exit strategy.

This index covers 84 days (Jun 10 – Sep 1 2026) of directly observed pump.fun trade data, ~53k graduated tokens. Mints outside that window show live liquidity data only, with reputation marked "unknown" — no invented numbers.

Built on top of a live Solana memecoin trading bot's rug-detection research. Source: github.com/cristiandkzk/rugscore.
